# Urban Air Quality Intelligence

## Comprehensive Environmental Data Science Platform

### 1. Project Overview

An end-to-end Environmental Data Science project studying
air quality patterns across Indian cities.

The project investigates pollutant distributions, AQI
relationships, seasonal trends, temporal patterns and
environmental risk indicators.

### 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze air quality and pollutant distributions.
- Examine missing data and data quality.
- Investigate pollutant-AQI relationships.
- Analyze seasonal and annual pollution patterns.
- Compare environmental conditions across cities.
- Identify persistent pollution hotspots.
- Examine extreme pollution observations.
- Develop a comparative environmental risk index.
- Perform statistical robustness analysis.

### 3. Dataset

**Dataset:** Air Quality Data in India

**Source:** Kaggle

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india

**Primary file:** city_day.csv

The original dataset contains approximately 29,531 rows
and 16 columns, covering 2015 through 2020-07-01.

### 8. Important Considerations

- Missing observations are not automatically treated as zero.
- The 2020 dataset covers only part of the year.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- City averages are not population-weighted exposure estimates.
- The composite environmental risk index is project-defined,
  not an official regulatory index.
- Extreme AQI values are retained and flagged for analysis.

### 9. Reproducibility
